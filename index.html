The right system.
For every moving part.

From individual lubrication points to complete installations,
we select and engineer solutions around your equipment,
lubricant requirements and operating conditions.

Centralized
Lubrication Systems

Deliver the right amount of lubricant to multiple points from one central source, reducing manual maintenance and keeping machinery running smoothly.

Oil-Air
Lubrication Systems

Deliver precisely metered oil through a controlled air stream, supporting reliable lubrication for high-speed bearings & spindles with minimal lubricant consumption.

Single Line
Lubrication Systems

Supply measured lubricant to multiple points through a single main line, offering a compact, adaptable solution for everyday machine lubrication.

Grease
Lubrication Systems

Deliver grease directly to critical components at controlled intervals, helping reduce friction, wear and maintenance demands in heavy-duty machinery.

Progressive
Distributor Systems

Distribute measured lubricant in a defined sequence, ensuring each connected point receives its share for consistent, controlled machine lubrication.

Oil Circulating
System

Continuously circulate, filter and cool lubricating oil to protect bearings and gears, remove heat and support reliable operation under demanding conditions.

Engineering and Custom Solutions

  1. 01 PLAN

    Every lubrication solution starts with understanding your equipment. We review the lubrication points, operating conditions and maintenance requirements to identify what your machinery needs. This helps us define a clear approach to system selection, layout and lubricant delivery.

    Understand the application

  2. 02 DESIGN

    We turn your requirements into a complete lubrication system. Our team develops the layout and selects pumps, distributors, controls and monitoring components suited to your application. Each design considers how the system will integrate with your machinery and how your team will access it for maintenance.

    Engineer the solution

  3. 03 INSTALL

    We help put your system into service with installation guidance, commissioning support and post-commissioning test-run assistance. Our team also provides customer training so your operators and maintenance personnel understand the system, its components and the checks needed during everyday operation.

    Bring it into operation

  4. 04 MAINTAIN

    Our support continues after installation. Through annual maintenance contracts and individual service visits, we help you care for your lubrication system throughout operation. When your equipment or requirements change, we also support the replacement of existing systems with solutions suited to your needs.

    Keep performance moving
