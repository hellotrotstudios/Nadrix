Our systems

Better control.
Less manual effort.

Moving from manual greasing to a centralized system changes how your machines are maintained — and how long they last.

  • Longer component life

    Consistent lubrication reduces friction and wear on bearings, gears and guideways.

  • Less unplanned downtime

    Points are lubricated while the machine runs — no stops for manual greasing.

  • Lower lubricant consumption

    Metered doses replace over-greasing, cutting lubricant use and waste.

  • Safer maintenance

    Technicians no longer need to reach hot, moving or hard-to-access points.
